# Sora Pack – 20-second TikTok Teaser (9:16)

Sora has no "negative prompt" field, so restrictions are written positively inside the prompt text. Check in the interface which maximum duration and resolution your plan offers, then choose Option A or B.

Settings: vertical 9:16 orientation, highest resolution offered (1080x1920 if available), duration depending on the option.

---

## Option A – a single prompt (if your max duration reaches 20 s; if it is 15 s, merge shots 3 and 4)

```
Vertical 9:16, 20-second faceless tabletop commercial, five shots with clean cuts, bright and clean look. Warm soft window light, a wooden table, deep navy background with warm amber accents. Only hands appear on camera; no faces, no full people. Smooth, slow camera moves. Subject stays in the middle of the frame, with generous empty space at the top and at the bottom.

Shot 1 (0-2s): A hand slides a small closed gift box across the wooden table into frame. Slight slow push-in.
Shot 2 (2-7s): Dark blue over-ear wireless headphones on the table. A hand lifts them slowly and rotates them; soft light glints on the ear cups.
Shot 3 (7-12s): Close-up, shallow depth of field. Two hands snap together a half-built orange building-brick sports-car model, with loose bricks around it.
Shot 4 (12-16s): A family guessing board game. An adult hand and a child's hand flip small illustrated character cards upright on the table, cheerful playful pace.
Shot 5 (16-20s): Calm static shot of the blue headphones, the orange brick car model and the board game arranged on the table. The camera slowly pulls back, leaving a clean empty area in the lower middle.

Audio: light upbeat instrumental music only, no vocals, no speech.
Everything is plain and unbranded: blank surfaces, no writing, no logos, no symbols. Hands are natural with five fingers each, motion is steady and smooth, lighting is stable with no flicker.
```

## Option B – 5 separate clips (more reliable for hands)

Generate each clip in Sora, then assemble them in CapCut. Suggested durations: 4 s, 5 s, 5 s, 4 s, 5 s (you will trim to the desired pace afterwards). Paste the prefix, then the shot prompt.

**Prefix (paste in front of every prompt)**
```
Vertical 9:16 faceless tabletop commercial, bright and clean look, warm soft window light, wooden table, deep navy background with warm amber accents. Only hands visible, no faces. Smooth camera movement, stable lighting. Subject in the middle of the frame with empty space above and below. Blank unbranded objects, no writing, no logos, no symbols. Natural hands with five fingers. No dialogue.
```

**Shot 1 (keep ~2 s)** – A hand slides a small closed gift box across the wooden table into frame, slight slow push-in. Hook shot.

**Shot 2 (~5 s)** – Dark blue over-ear wireless headphones resting on the wooden table. A hand lifts them slowly and rotates them; soft light glints on the ear cups.

**Shot 3 (~5 s)** – Close-up with shallow depth of field. Two hands snap together a half-built orange building-brick sports-car model, loose bricks scattered around it.

**Shot 4 (~4 s)** – A family guessing board game on the table. An adult hand and a child's hand flip small illustrated character cards upright, cheerful playful pace.

**Shot 5 (~5 s)** – Calm static shot of the blue headphones, the orange brick car model and the board game arranged on the table. The camera slowly pulls back. Clean empty area in the lower middle of the frame.

**Music**: add it in the edit rather than in Sora, to keep a single continuous track across the 5 cuts.

## Sora tips
- Use "Remix" to fix one specific flaw (e.g. "keep everything, make the hand slower") instead of starting over.
- If hands look distorted: simplify the gesture, shorten the clip, or generate 2–3 variants and keep the best one.
- The words "kid" / "child's hand" may be filtered: if shot 4 is refused, try "a smaller hand" or "a young person's hand".
- For shot 4, avoid describing recognizable characters on the cards: "simple colorful cartoon animals" works better.
- Text, logos, prices and the "Prime Big Deal" mention: add them in TikTok / CapCut, in the top (15%) and bottom (20%) areas left empty.
- Final export: 1080x1920, 30 fps. If Sora outputs 24 fps, let CapCut convert on export.
